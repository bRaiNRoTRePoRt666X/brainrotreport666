# Community

Builds and maintains relationships with the audience and partners. Full role, KPIs and tools: [`docs/departments/community.md`](../../docs/departments/community.md).

| Folder | Holds | Cadence |
|--------|-------|---------|
| `engagement/` | Comment responses, community posts, live interaction reports | Weekly |
| `moderation/` | Moderation logs, guideline enforcement, crisis notes | Daily |
| `feedback/` | Survey results, comment analysis, feature requests | Weekly |
| `collaborations/` | Partner, guest and cross-promotion tracker | Ongoing |

## Handoffs
- **From Distribution:** publish notification received, engagement plan ready, moderation guidelines set.
- **To Creative:** audience feedback, documented content requests, collaboration opportunities.

## Conventions
- Collaborations that involve payment, gifts or promotion must be disclosed. Record them in the tracker and tell `legal-compliance`.
- Do not store personal data about individual community members beyond what moderation needs. See `legal-compliance/privacy/`.
