## Body

### Beat 1: 33 YEARS. THREE LIFE SENTENCES.
`4-20s`

WJLA’s Nick Minock reported Thursday’s hearing. One man had served 33 years of three life sentences for killing a couple in their seventies after cutting their power and breaking in. Their grandson asked the board to keep him in. The board granted parole.

*Visual:* One case card. No name unless you are reading it off the WJLA story on screen. No generated face.

### Beat 2: VICTIMS SAID NO
`20-36s`

The same meeting granted parole to a bank robber who had shot at people, a man who had served 28 years for stabbing a relative, and three people convicted of rape. One of them had not taken the treatment programs. Victims had asked the board to say no. 7News also lists child abuse among the convictions at that meeting.

*Visual:* Three more lines: bank robbery, a stabbing after 28 years, three rape convictions. One of those three had not taken the treatment programs, according to 7News.

### Beat 3: 48 OF 205
`36-49s`

Since May, 7News counted 48 grants out of 205 cases, almost one in four. The previous board granted fewer than 2 in 100. Minock also reported an inmate thanked this board and told them to have a safe day.

*Visual:* The season rate beside the old one: almost 1 in 4, against under 2 in 100. Then the safe-day line, attributed to Minock.

## Call to Action

Pinned comment: Eight grants in one meeting, after victims asked for no. Is the new board applying the statute, or cutting sentences the court already handed down?

## Do Not Say

- Do not say seven murderers were released. The Thursday meeting was 8 grants out of 10. The convictions across that group include murder, rape, robbery, and child abuse. They are not seven murder cases.
- Do not say they all walked out that afternoon if you cannot show the release paperwork. 7News reported the board released them. Grant and the day they leave custody are not always the same hour.
- Do not generate mugshots or stamp PAROLED across a face.
- Minock’s first post that day said 7 of 8 so far. The later 7News story, after the meeting, is 8 of 10. Use the later count.
