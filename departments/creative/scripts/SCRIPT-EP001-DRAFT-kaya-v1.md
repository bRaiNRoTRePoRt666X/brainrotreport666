## Body

### Beat 1: SOLD BEFORE THE BREAK
`4-18s`

On September twenty-sixth, opposition spokesman Zeynel Emre alleged Fatma Betül Sayan Kaya and her husband put a hundred and sixty-three million lira into shipbuilder Özata Denizcilik in April, then sold before the September sixteenth crash for about two-point-two billion lira. Reuters and Turkish Minute carried it as an allegation. She has not been convicted.

*Visual:* thumb-break.png. Push down the long red candle.

### Beat 2: ₺163 MILLION IN / ₺2.2 BILLION OUT
`18-34s`

She quit her ruling-party posts that weekend. Istanbul prosecutors then froze the couple’s assets. A financial paper reported they wired roughly two-point-two billion lira to a Treasury account under a remorse rule. The regulator’s first criminal complaints on this stock named eleven other people.

*Visual:* thumb-trade.png. Push into the bow.

### Beat 3: ASSETS FROZEN
`34-48s`

The fund collapse around those trades locked a hundred and thirty-one funds and hit hundreds of thousands of investors. Her alleged trade is one slice. Paying the money back is not the same as being questioned.

*Visual:* thumb-freeze.png. Push into the gap in the door. Optional extra line, Milli Gazete only: after the resignation she was removed from the party executive WhatsApp group.

- Do not say she drained 2 billion dollars. The allegation is about 2.2 billion lira, roughly 45 million dollars in sale proceeds.
- Do not say citizens lost everything to her. The fund liquidation is a separate figure, on the order of 19 billion dollars across 131 funds.
- Do not say nothing happened. She resigned, prosecutors froze the couple’s assets, and a paper reported a Treasury payment.
- Do not call her a fraudster. She has not been convicted. The first Özata criminal complaints named eleven other people.
